About the practice

Less effort. More listening.

Developed by Vanda Scaravelli, this approach treats the spine less like a rigid pole and more like a wave — something that lengthens and curves as you breathe, rather than something to be held in place. There's no pushing into a "correct" shape. Instead, we use breath, gravity and patience to let the body soften and open on its own terms.

I'm a Brighton-based teacher trained in the Scaravelli lineage, running small classes by the sea for people who want yoga without the performance — whatever your age, flexibility or experience.

Scaravelli-lineage trained · Groups of 8 or fewer · All bodies welcome
